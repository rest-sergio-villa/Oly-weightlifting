# Mon 21 – Wed 23 Sep — lunch & dinner plan

Built after the Food4Fitness box was stolen on 18 Sep. The reorder can't land
before Fri 25, so these three days are cooked at home.

**Constraint that shaped it:** lunch is personal (batch-prepped Sunday night,
eaten cold or microwaved, can be as adventurous as it likes), dinner is shared
with Gus (one pan, conventional, portioned unevenly at the table).

The three lunches come off **one chicken cook-up** — 600g raw breast, poached or
pan-roasted plain on Sunday, then split three ways and finished with a different
sauce each day. Same effort as one meal, three different cuisines.

## Monday 21 — rest

**Lunch — shawarma chicken couscous bowl.** Chicken breast 100g cooked tossed in
baharat/cumin/smoked paprika, 90g dry couscous (cooked), chopped
cucumber–tomato–parsley–lemon salad 150g, 60g Greek yoghurt with sumac,
¼ avocado.

**Dinner (shared) — spaghetti with tuna, olive and caper tomato sauce.** Uses the
two tins already in the house. For two: 250g dry spaghetti, 2 tins tuna,
350g passata, onion, garlic, chilli, 40g kalamata olives, capers, 2 tbsp olive oil.

Sergio's plate: **150g dry spaghetti, 1 tin of the tuna, about 2 tsp of the oil,
10g parmesan**, plus 150g frozen veg on the side. Gus takes the rest.

| Day total | 2,828 | 164 | 353 | 78 |
| Target | 2,760 | 160 | 350 | 80 |

## Tuesday 22 — Oly Day 1 (pm)

**Lunch — bún gà.** Cold rice vermicelli 100g dry, lemongrass chicken breast 150g
cooked (1 tsp oil in the marinade), shredded carrot, cucumber, lettuce, mint and
coriander 150g, 50ml nuoc cham, 10g crushed peanuts. Travels better than any hot
lunch and is almost fat-free, which leaves room for the salmon at night.

**Dinner (shared) — teriyaki salmon, rice, greens.** For two: 2 salmon fillets,
soy/mirin/honey glaze, jasmine rice, broccoli and green beans.

Sergio's plate: **130g salmon, 180g cooked rice, 180g greens, ~40ml glaze.**

| | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day total | 2,895 | 156 | 374 | 77 |
| Target | 2,860 | 160 | 375 | 80 |

Salmon is deliberate — it carries 17g of the day's fat in one item, which is the
gap the F4F meals used to leave open.

## Wednesday 23 — 6am class

Tightest day of the three: the 83g pre-feed and the post-class smoothie together
put 1,600 kcal down before 9am, so lunch and dinner share only ~1,260.

**Lunch — gochujang chicken rice bowl.** 200g cooked jasmine rice, 150g cooked
chicken breast in 30g gochujang sauce, 60g kimchi, cucumber, a soft-boiled egg,
5g sesame seeds. Eat cold or warm; the kimchi is the point.

**Dinner (shared) — shakshuka.** For two: 2×400g tinned tomatoes, onion,
2 capsicums, cumin and paprika, 1–2 tbsp olive oil, 4 eggs, bread to mop.
Straight from the pan, portioned by how much bread each person takes.

Sergio's plate: **2 eggs, half the sauce, 120g sourdough.**

| | kcal | P | C | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day total | 2,935 | 152 | 371 | 82 |
| Target | 2,860 | 160 | 375 | 80 |

Protein lands 8g light. A protein water in the afternoon covers it if the day
feels short; otherwise leave it — the rolling average is running above 160.
